DC Calculator

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

DC Calculator is an offline calculator app. This policy explains how the standalone Android app handles information.

Information stored by the app

The app stores recent calculation history, the calculator memory value, and the selected light/dark theme locally on your device using app storage. These values are used only to provide calculator features.

Collection and sharing

The standalone Android app does not require an account, does not request sensitive device permissions, and does not transmit calculator entries to our servers. The app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or in-app purchases.

Retention and deletion

History can be cleared using the app's Clear History control. You can remove other saved app data through Android Settings or uninstall the app.

Children

The app is a general-purpose calculator and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes

If the app's data practices change in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support email listed on the DC Calculator Google Play Store page.

This policy describes the standalone offline Android app. Any future online or third-party features will require this policy to be reviewed and updated as applicable.

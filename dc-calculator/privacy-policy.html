DC Calculator

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

DC Calculator is a calculator web app with optional VIP membership requests and an optional passphrase-protected private vault. The calculator and vault work on the current device; VIP status checks and submitted membership requests use a secure Supabase service.

Calculator data

Calculation history, calculator memory, colour theme and related preferences are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not uploaded as part of normal calculation use. You can clear history in the app or remove site data in your browser settings.

Private Vault

If you are an active VIP member, the Private Vault can store your selected photos and files in this browser's IndexedDB storage. Before saving, the app encrypts file contents and file names with AES-GCM 256-bit encryption using a key derived from your separate vault passphrase with PBKDF2-SHA-256. The vault data is not uploaded to our servers. The passphrase is not stored by the app.

Use a strong, unique passphrase and keep separate copies of important originals. If you forget the passphrase, clear browser data, uninstall a browser/PWA, or lose the device, the encrypted files may be unrecoverable. This web version does not provide fingerprint or face unlock; it uses the passphrase you create.

VIP membership requests

The current VIP request workflow is manual; this app does not process card, UPI or bank payments automatically. If you submit a membership request after making a transfer arranged privately with the developer, we send the email address you entered, selected plan, transaction/UTR reference and app-install identifier to the Supabase service. The service hashes the install identifier before storing it. The request remains pending until the developer independently checks receipt and approves or rejects it.

Do not enter banking passwords, card PINs, CVV, OTPs, or other authentication secrets. The app does not ask for them. The developer may review the submitted email, plan and payment reference to verify a pending request. There is no automatic renewal in this manual workflow.

Service providers and retention

Supabase hosts the VIP membership status and request records. Those records are retained for membership verification, abuse prevention, and request review. Encrypted vault files remain in the local browser database until you delete them or clear that browser's site data. A transaction reference and email you submit for membership are server-side records and are not removed by clearing local browser data.

Children and advertising

DC Calculator does not require an account for ordinary calculator use. The current version does not include an advertising SDK or analytics SDK. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the information a user voluntarily enters into a VIP request.

Security limits

Encryption protects vault files at rest within browser storage, but no web app can guarantee protection if the device or browser is compromised, malicious extensions can read the page, or someone watches you unlock the vault. Lock the vault when you finish and protect your device account.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if data practices change. For privacy questions or a membership request, contact the app developer through the official channel that provided the app to you.

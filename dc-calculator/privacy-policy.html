DC Calculator

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy applies to the DC Calculator website/PWA and the standalone Android app. Calculator features run on the user's device. VIP membership features use the additional data flows described below.

Calculator data

Calculation history, calculator memory, and theme preferences are stored locally on the device/browser. They are not uploaded to DC Calculator servers by the calculator.

VIP membership requests on the website

Until an automated payment gateway is configured, the website does not take or process a payment. If a user independently makes an actual transfer after receiving payment instructions from the developer, the VIP request form may send their email address, chosen plan, an installation identifier hash, and the payment reference/UTR to the DC Calculator VIP service for manual review. A request stays pending until the developer checks receipt; submitting a reference does not by itself activate VIP. Do not enter a bank/card PIN, password, CVV, OTP or full bank account details in the app.

Google Play subscriptions

In the Android app, subscriptions are intended to be processed through Google Play Billing once monthly and yearly subscription products are configured in Play Console. Google Play manages checkout and subscription payments under its own terms and privacy policy. If checkout says a product is unavailable, the app has not taken a payment. The app does not collect or store a user's card details.

Private Vault

The optional VIP Private Vault encrypts files and note contents on the device using a passphrase-derived AES-GCM encryption key before saving encrypted records to local browser/app storage. File names and file type metadata are encrypted along with the contents. The vault does not upload vault photos or files to DC Calculator servers. On the Android app, device authentication (fingerprint, face or device screen-lock credential where supported) is an additional access check; the vault passphrase is still required. The browser version uses the vault passphrase.

Keep backups of original files. Forgetting the vault passphrase or clearing/uninstalling the local app/browser data can permanently remove access to encrypted vault content. The developer cannot reset or recover a vault passphrase.

Data retention and deletion

Local calculator and vault data can be removed by clearing the app/browser data or uninstalling the app; vault content should be backed up first. VIP request and membership records are retained for membership review, validity, and basic payment records. For a membership-data correction or deletion request, contact the developer through the support contact published on the official app page.

Permissions and sharing

The standalone calculator does not need contacts, location, microphone, camera or SMS permissions. Internet access is used for website membership-status/request features and Google Play Billing in the Android version. DC Calculator does not sell personal information or include advertising/analytics SDKs in this version.

Children

DC Calculator is a general-purpose calculator and does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond information a user actively enters into a VIP membership request.

Changes

This policy will be updated if app features or data practices change. Do not use VIP payments while the app's checkout screen says payments are unavailable.
